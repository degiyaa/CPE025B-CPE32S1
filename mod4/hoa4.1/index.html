Software Design 2

This course introduces students to front-end web development using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It covers the essentials of structuring web content, styling and layout for responsive and accessible interfaces, and implementing client-side behavior through programming constructs and object-based JavaScript.

Feedback and Control Systems

The course deals with the analysis and design of feedback control systems. Emphasis is on the achievement of three major objectives: producing the desired transient response, reducing the steady-state error and achieving stability.

Cloud Management and Security

This course gives a broad overview of cloud security and allows students to gain critical insights into issues such as data security, key management, and identity and access management. As organizations migrate to the cloud, they need cloud-savvy information security professionals.

Introduction to Intellectual Property

Introduces students to general concepts and principles Intellectual Property. It starts with Intellectual Property Rights. Focus is on understanding of different Intellectual properties such as patents; inventions and industrial designs; copyright; trademarks and creative problem solving.

Human-Centered Innovation

This course introduces students to a practical, human-centered approach to solving complex problems in technology and society. Inspired by Enterprise Design Thinking, the course equips students with the mindset and methods to design with empathy, collaborate across diverse teams, and create innovative solutions that prioritize human needs, sustainability, and ethical responsibility.
