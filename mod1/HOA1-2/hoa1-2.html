A Recipe to making Bulalo

Stews and Soups: Bulalo

Tom Yum Goong is a classic Thai soup renowned for its bold flavors and aromatic spices. This hot and sour soup features a tantalizing blend of lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves, combined with succulent shrimp.

The key to a delicious Tom Yum Goong lies in the balance of flavors – sweet, sour, salty, and spicy.

- Chef Somchai, Thai Cuisine Expert

Ingredients:

  • Beef Shank: 2 lbs
  • Cabbage: 1/2, whole leaf individually detached
  • Bok Choy: 1 bundle
  • Corn Cobs: 2, each cut to 3 parts
  • Whole Peppercorn: 2 Tablespoons
  • Green Onions: 1/2 cup, cut into 1 1/2 inch pieces
  • Onion: 1
  • Water: 34 oz.
  • Fish Sauce: Optional

Preparation:

  1. In a big cooking pot, pour in water and bring to a boil.
  2. Put-in the beef shank followed by the onion and whole pepper corn then simmer for 1.5hrs (30mins if using a pressure cooker) or until meat is tender.
  3. Add the corn and simmer for another 10mins.
  4. Add the fish sauce, cabbage, bok choy, and green onion.
  5. Serve hot. Share and Enjoy!

Cooking Tips:

  • If you're using a pot, let the meat simmer for about an hour and a half.
  • 2 tablespoons of fish sauce for a better taste.
